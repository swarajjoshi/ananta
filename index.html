Before we begin

Your camera helps ANANTA recognise your hand gestures and count each repetition. Video stays on your device; no microphone is used.

Keep your hand comfortably inside the frame and bring your thumb and index fingertip together naturally. Good light helps. To finish, touch your thumb to your middle fingertip (the Sacred Circle), keep the other fingers extended, and hold for two seconds. The Finish Session button always works too.
